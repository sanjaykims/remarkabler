# Screenshots checklist

Drop **PNG** files into this folder with the exact names below — the main
[`README.md`](../../README.md) gallery will then render them on GitHub.

## What to capture

Phone-portrait screenshots are great (the app is mobile-first). Crop or
mosaic out anything personal you'd rather not share publicly.

| File | What it should show |
|---|---|
| `chat.png` | The **Chat** tab with one or two exchanges that look like *you* asking your companion something personal and getting a thoughtful answer. The most magnetic screenshot — make it count. |
| `memory.png` | The **Memory** tab with a few lines of the "profile of you" visible. Demonstrates the unique angle (accumulated understanding, not just notes). |
| `notebooks.png` | The **Notebooks** tab with one or two entries — ideally a mix of `Transcribing…` and `✓` to show the background loop. |
| `cost.png` | The **Cost** tab calendar showing real numbers — transparency is a trust win, not a deterrent. |

## Tips

- Use **dark mode** if your daily reMarkable workflow is dark — it photographs more cleanly than light + flash.
- Aim for ~1080 px wide; smaller is fine (GitHub scales them).
- If a screenshot is identifying, blur the sensitive bits in your phone's photo editor before saving.
- More than four is fine — add them to the gallery in `README.md` if you do.
